Full legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

You may share and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercially, if you give
appropriate credit (cite this dataset, see `CITATION.cff`), link to the license, and
indicate changes.

The numbers in the dataset were reported in the cited papers. Each record gives the
paper's DOI, its license and where the values appear. No text, tables or figures are
copied from the papers. Please also cite the original paper when you use a record.
